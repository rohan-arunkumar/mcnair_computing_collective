McNair Computing Collective

Founded in September 2024, the McNair Computing Collective is a student organization with over 50 members. The club provides instructional sessions and collaborative research activities centered on computer science topics.

Objective

Accessibility in Computer Science

The primary purpose of this club is to make computer science instruction and technical research processes more accessible to students within the high school community.

To coordinate these activities, our internal structure includes dedicated student departments managing education, community outreach, and social media channels.

Internal Education

We organize peer-led instructional sessions for McNair High School students, focusing on the fundamentals of programming, machine learning methodologies, and application design pipelines.

Outreach Initiatives

The club organizes a city-wide research symposium intended for younger individuals and underserved demographics. These events are funded through independent student fundraising campaigns.
